🌾 Agronomic cycle — stage-by-stage AI advisory Sowing → Harvest

    Activity level from satellite & climatic data across the 10-month cycle. Red markers = the six key agronomic stages where the platform delivers targeted recommendations.

    1Sowing date optimization

    Pick the optimal sowing date from agro-climatic conditions — satellite baseline on soil moisture, temperature & vegetation cover; forecasts avoid frost and excessive rainfall.

    • Agro-climatic data integration (historical + real-time)
    • Weather forecasting for sowing windows
    • Baseline soil moisture & vegetation cover maps
    🤖 AI: Optimal sowing window opens in ~10 days. Soil moisture at 61% field capacity, frost risk low — proceed with pre-plant irrigation 5 days prior.

    2Fertilization strategy · Round 1

    Ideal first-round schedule from satellite crop-health imagery and weather — applied only when conditions maximize uptake, never lost to rain or wind.

    • Remote-sensing nutrient-deficiency detection
    • Localized doses by soil, crop & zone
    • Weather-timed application windows
    🤖 AI: 34% of command area is in the Minimum nutrition zone. Variable-rate prescription map ready — target SE blocks first, avoid Maximum zones (waste risk).

    3Pest, disease & weed · Round 1

    AI analysis of satellite imagery catches infestations and weed growth before visible symptoms — with alerts and sustainable treatment options.

    • AI + computer vision early detection
    • Real-time alerts via mobile channels
    • Organic options & weather-aware treatment timing
    🤖 AI: Early stress signature in Zone C (NDVI −0.08 vs baseline). Inspect within 48h — recommend spot-treatment, not blanket spray.

    4Fertilization strategy · Round 2

    Second round adjusted against Round 1 results — evaluated from satellite response, current growth stage and weather to prevent runoff.

    • Round 1 impact evaluation via NDVI response
    • Growth-stage tailored nutrient plan
    • Over-fertilization & runoff prevention
    🤖 AI: Round 1 produced +22% NDVI response in Zone A. Reduce Round 2 dose by 15% in Zone B; maintain plan elsewhere.

    5Pest, disease & weed · Round 2

    Continuous monitoring through crop development — targeted late-stage treatments localized by geography, pest type and crop vulnerability.

    • Ongoing satellite surveillance
    • Precision second-round treatment
    • Localized by pest type & vulnerability
    🤖 AI: No new outbreak signatures. Maintain twice-weekly monitoring cadence; wind Tuesday may spread spores — avoid field work.

    6Yield estimation · pre-harvest

    AI yield prediction from plant height, leaf colour & density — plus protein/humidity quality estimates and market price forecasting for post-harvest planning.

    • AI yield prediction model
    • Protein & humidity quality estimates
    • Market price forecasting
    🤖 AI: Estimated yield 4.8 t/ha · protein 12.1% · humidity 11.8%. Indicative price ETB 41–44/kg — align post-harvest logistics & cold chain now.
